Why Work With Hears Hearing & Hearables

Most online hearing aid stores are retail operations without clinical staff. Most audiology clinics do not operate online stores. We do both because we think the combination produces a better experience than either one alone.

Our clinic, Hears to U in Hopkins, Minnesota, has been fitting and caring for patients for more than 30 years. Kim Fishman is the audiologist behind both the clinic and this store. She and other networked providers fit hearing aids in person or via telehealth for patients across the country.

Our store is membership based, so if you join, you can save money and earn rewards. Membership is very affordable for one year.

We are independent, not owned by a manufacturer, and pride ourselves on doing what is best for you. We have been offering demos of hearing aids in our Minnesota clinic since 2012. We are now offering the ability to demo one to three hearing aids remotely.

We carry prescription hearing aids from eight independent manufacturers, not a single franchise brand, not a big box store's house brand, and not private label devices that can only be serviced by one retail chain. Every brand we carry can be programmed by any hearing provider with the right software, which matters when you travel, when you move, or when you simply want options for your long term care.

We publish our prices. For the two brands that prohibit online price display, Oticon and Phonak, we explain why and invite you to contact us directly. Transparent pricing is not just a policy here. It is the reason we built the store the way we did.

Best of all, when you contact us with a question, you are reaching a clinical team, not a customer service script. We do not offer our phone number readily. We want to save personal information for our patients. If you want to become a patient of ours, we will give you our phone number. Emails are free. Service does cost a small amount.
